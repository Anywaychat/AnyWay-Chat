My friend is blind. Every time he wants to send a message, he asks his parents to type it for him. We built AnyWay so he never has to ask again.

The founder’s reason for building AnyWay

You talk your way. They talk theirs.

Each person picks how they send and how they receive, once. After that every message, call and conversation is translated automatically.

You send

by signing, speaking, tapping a phrase, typing or using braille keys.

AnyWay translates

everything into text, then into the other person’s way, in about a second.

They receive

as text and signs, a natural voice, live captions or vibration patterns.

Wherever you need to talk.

Chat

Messages and family groups. Each one arrives in the form you can use, with the original sign or voice kept alongside.

Voice calls

Signs and taps are spoken aloud. Speech becomes live captions.

Video calls

Sign on camera and be heard. See their words as captions and signs.

Go mode

At a shop, a clinic or a bus stop, put your phone between you. It interprets both ways, and the other person needs no app.
