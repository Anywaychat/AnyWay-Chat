Open AnyWay

Chat was never built for everyone. We're changing that.

Most apps assume you can see the screen, hear a voice note and type a reply. Millions of people can't. AnyWay lets blind, deaf, deaf-blind and non-speaking people chat, call and meet anyone, in their own way.

Meet the people AnyWay is for

Everyday chat quietly shuts them out. Here's what that feels like, and what changes.

A
Arjunis blind

Every message goes through someone else. His mum reads them out and types his replies, even the private ones.

With AnyWay, he just talks. He says "Hey Chat, tell Priya I'm on my way", and it's sent. Replies are read to him in a natural voice.

Arjun says"Hey Chat, any new messages?"
AnyWay replies, out loud "Priya sent you a message: coming down now."

And for everyone who talks with them

Family, friends, the shopkeeper, the doctor. They don't learn sign language or braille, and they don't change how they talk. They chat and call like on any app. AnyWay translates in between, so each person gets the message in the way they understand.
