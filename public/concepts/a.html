Most apps assume you can see, hear and type.

Hundreds of millions of people can’t do all three. Today they wait for a parent, a friend or an interpreter to help them talk. AnyWay removes the wait.

430Mpeople live with disabling hearing loss (WHO)
70M+deaf people use a sign language (WFD)
97Mpeople can’t rely on speech to be understood
<500certified sign interpreters for India’s ~18M deaf people

You talk your way. They talk theirs.

Every message becomes text in the middle, then reaches each person in the form they can use.

You send by

  • Signing to the camera
  • Speaking
  • Tapping ready phrases
  • Typing or braille
AnyWay translates

They receive as

  • Text and sign animations
  • A natural voice
  • Live captions
  • Vibration patterns

Made for five ways of talking.

Blind

Speaks to send. Hears every reply. No parent needed to type.

Deaf

Signs to the camera. Sees text and signs.

Non-speaking

Taps a phrase. The app speaks for them.

Deaf-blind

Types in braille. Feels replies as vibration.

Everyone else

Chats and talks normally. Learns nothing new.

Chat, calls and real life.

Video calls
