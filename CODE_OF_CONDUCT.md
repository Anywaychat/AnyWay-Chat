# Code of Conduct

AnyWay Chat is built with and for people with disabilities. We want everyone who takes part (users, testers, contributors and maintainers) to feel welcome and respected.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make taking part in this project free from harassment for everyone, whatever their disability, age, body, ethnicity, caste, religion, gender identity or expression, sexual orientation, language, level of experience, education or background.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient. People communicate in different ways and at different speeds: by signing, speaking, typing, tapping or with assistive technology.
- Use respectful words. Say "deaf", "blind", "can't speak" or "non-speaking" and "older people"; never use insulting terms.
- Listen to people with lived experience. When a deaf, blind or non-speaking person says something doesn't work for them, take it seriously.
- Give and accept feedback about the work, not the person.
- Make your own contributions accessible: describe screenshots, caption videos, and write in plain language.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, mocking someone's disability, language or way of communicating
- Sexual language or images, or unwanted attention
- Sharing anyone's private information without their permission
- Trolling, threats, or encouraging anyone else to do these things

## Enforcement

Maintainers will remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code and issues that break this code, and may warn, suspend or ban anyone who does so.

To report a problem, contact the maintainers privately through the [organisation's GitHub page](https://github.com/Anywaychat), or open a [private security advisory](https://github.com/Anywaychat/AnyWay-Chat/security/advisories/new) if it involves personal data. Reports are kept confidential.
