865 million people are left out of chat apps built for one way of talking.

Today a blind teenager waits for a parent to type his messages. A deaf customer writes on paper at the counter. A grandmother whose eyes and ears are fading stops reading the family group. AnyWay ends all three.

43Mpeople are blind
295Mlive with moderate-to-severe vision impairment
430Mlive with disabling hearing loss
97Mcould benefit from picture and voice aids (AAC) to talk
865Mpeople with vision, hearing or speech needs

Total adds the blind, vision, hearing and AAC estimates (43 + 295 + 430 + 97 million). Sources: IAPB Vision Atlas (blind, vision impairment), WHO 2026 (hearing loss), Beukelman & Light, via ASHA (AAC).

And it grows with age: over 25% of people over 60 have disabling hearing loss, and most people with vision impairment are over 50. India has 149 million people over 60, and will have 347 million by 2050. Sources: WHO (hearing, vision), UNFPA India Ageing Report 2023.
